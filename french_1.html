Video

Here's the video again, for the sake of convenience:

Go ahead and watch the video again, but instead of trying to point out words that you recognize, I'd like you to try something else. I want you to try to focus as much on you can as the sounds they're making with the words they're saying, and I want you to read the French captions. Answer the Question

You're going to do some self study. Being part of a language class is a great privilege, but self-study is an important part of language learning, especially after you graduate.

I want you to figure out what this question means, and I want you to start learning a basic way that you specifically would respond to it. Here's the question:

Qu'est ce qui te rend content(e) ?

Use any tool you have at your disposal to translate and then answer this question. Make sure your answer...

  • ...is simple
  • ...is grammatically correct
  • ...and makes sense

Here are some great tools you might want to use. Don't just use one, explore them all!

  • Google Translate
  • DeepL
  • Reverso
  • Linguee
  • WordReference

Once you've found a simple response to this question unique to you, spend a few minutes asking each other (ask at least 5 different people!) this question and answering it. Feel free to explain not just to your classmates but also to your wonderful substitute what exactly your answer means, why it's important to you, and how the way you say it in French is different than the way you say it in English.

Include in your email:

  • What the question means in English

Interview Questions

So far this year we've learned 5 questions. They are:

  1. Comment ca va ?
  2. Comment tu t'appelles ?
  3. Qu'est-ce que tu aimes faire ?
  4. Ou tu habites ?
  5. Tu parles francais ?

Recall that we're getting ready for Entretien at the end of the quarter. Entretien is the French word for Interview, and that's just what we'll be doing.

To get some practice, please do the following:

  1. Pair up
  2. Assign one person to be the interviewer and one the interviewee
  3. The interviewer will ask each question, and the interviewee will answer them
  4. Once you've gone through them all, switch roles
  5. If you finish before five minutes are up, you can either keep going switching roles back and forth, or pick one question and write down as many answers to it as possible.

Video

This is a video from the YouTube channel Easy French, a subsidiary of the Easy Languages brand. This channel is a phenomenal resource if you want to learn French. It's a great source of audio input and cultural insight. There are also plenty of techniques you can use while watching to improve pronunciation and learn words and phrases, such as shadowing.

Go ahead and watch the video. If anybody manages to get Clevershare working, then watch it together. Otherwise, everybody can watch individually (but at the same time) on their Chromebooks with low but individually audible volume or headphones of some kind.
